# Tidebee: AI animated video maker

**[Tidebee](https://www.tidebee.com/) is an AI animated video maker that turns a script or topic into a 2D, 3D, whiteboard, explainer or AI video, in a ready-made style or one you create yourself.**

Paste a script or type a topic, pick a style, and get an animated video with voiceover that stays in sync with each scene. Tidebee runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install.

**[Make your first video](https://app.tidebee.com/signup?ref=github-readme)** · [How it works](https://www.tidebee.com/how-it-works/) · [Pricing](https://www.tidebee.com/pricing/) · [FAQ](https://www.tidebee.com/faq/)

## What you can make

| Style | Good for |
|---|---|
| [Whiteboard animation](https://www.tidebee.com/ai-whiteboard-animation-maker/) | Step-by-step explanations of a process or concept |
| [Doodle video](https://www.tidebee.com/ai-doodle-video-maker/) | Friendly, hand-drawn training and onboarding videos |
| [2D animated explainer](https://www.tidebee.com/ai-2d-animated-explainer-video-maker/) | A character with a problem and a product that solves it |

| [3D animation](https://www.tidebee.com/ai-3d-animation-video-maker/) | Physical products seen from every side |

You can also create your own style, make AI video, and make promo videos for physical products. Tidebee includes a video editor. Compare all the options on the [video styles](https://www.tidebee.com/video-styles/) page.

## How it works

1. **Start with words.** Paste a finished script, or type a topic if you have no script yet.
2. **Pick a style.** Choose a ready-made style or one you created.
3. **Generate and review.** Tidebee produces the animated video with synced voiceover. Watch it through and check every fact before you share it.

The full walkthrough is on the [how it works](https://www.tidebee.com/how-it-works/) page.

## Pricing

Three monthly plans, priced in US dollars. There is no free plan.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $15 a month | Up to 10 videos a month |
| Creator | $30 a month | Unlimited videos, social media scheduler, automatic social media uploads |
| Team | $50 a month | Everything in Creator, team collaboration, $10 of AI tokens every month |

Every video also uses AI tokens, about $2 a video. See [Tidebee pricing](https://www.tidebee.com/pricing/) for worked examples, or the [explainer video cost guide](https://www.tidebee.com/explainer-video-cost/) for what studios and freelancers publish as their prices.
